Fusor Project

I tried to build an inertial electrostatic confinement (IEC) fusor. It did not work. I did not achieve fusion, and I have no verified neutron measurements.

Status
Past experimental project
Dates
July 2024 to January 2025
Technologies
Vacuum systems, electrical systems, fabrication
Links
Read the retrospective

The project involved high-voltage equipment and other hazardous systems. This page is a high-level retrospective. It is not a build or operating guide, and I won’t publish wiring, voltages, gas or chemical procedures, or chamber construction details.

What I worked on

  • Researching how IEC fusors are designed.
  • Choosing and sourcing components.
  • Fabricating parts.
  • Troubleshooting the vacuum equipment and the electrical subsystems.

Where it got difficult

The gap between the design on paper and a working system showed up in:

  • Component selection.
  • Fabrication accuracy.
  • Fittings and sealing.
  • Vacuum equipment.
  • Electrical subsystems.

Next

  • Carry the lessons into future projects: check part ratings first, test each part on its own, and ask for help earlier.
