ThermoSim

ThermoSim simulates how temperature changes over time inside a solid wall. I’m building it to understand heat conduction numerically instead of only through textbook equations.

Status
In progress
Technologies
Python, NumPy, Matplotlib

This is a learning project. It hasn’t been validated against reference solutions, so I don’t treat its output as engineering results.

What I worked on

  • Defining material properties: density, specific heat capacity and thermal conductivity.
  • Representing the wall as a row of spatial nodes.
  • Calculating the temperature rate at each node.
  • Stepping the temperature distribution forward in time and recording the history.

What I’d do next

  • Plot the temperature distribution with Matplotlib.
  • Learn more about numerical stability before trusting any results.
